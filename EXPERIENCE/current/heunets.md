## Scope of Work

### Marketing Strategy

- Support the development and execution of the company's digital marketing strategy.
- Translate technical IT services into buyer-focused marketing messages.
- Research target audiences, buyer needs, and relevant market conversations.
- Develop content and channel strategies aligned with business objectives.
- Connect marketing activity with business-development and pipeline objectives.

### B2B Content & Positioning

- Develop LinkedIn content for the company and executive profiles.
- Create content around enterprise IT delivery, infrastructure, service delivery, field execution, and operational reliability.
- Build messaging around the problems faced by MSPs, service-delivery leaders, vendor managers, procurement teams, and operations leaders.
- Develop content designed to build credibility and recognition with the target audience.

### Marketing Operations

- Build and document marketing workflows.
- Create briefs, processes, reporting structures, and accountability systems.
- Coordinate activities across multiple digital channels.
- Track implementation and execution.
- Organize recurring marketing activities into structured workflows.
- Support collaboration and task delegation across the marketing function.

### Channel Management

Work across channels including:

- LinkedIn
- Google Business Profile
- Pinterest
- Website / SEO
- Other digital channels supporting discovery and lead generation

### Team Coordination

Coordinate and provide execution oversight for supporting team members, including delegation, task direction, quality review, and accountability.

### Reporting & Measurement

Track marketing activity against business movement rather than publication volume alone.

Relevant measures include:

- Qualified enquiries
- Discovery calls
- Landing-page conversions
- Channel-influenced opportunities
- Audience growth within the target market
- Executive-profile responses
- Search visibility
- Progression from awareness to conversation
