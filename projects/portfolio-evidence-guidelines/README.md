# Portfolio Evidence Guidelines

Use this checklist when preparing screenshots, notebook exports, transcripts, or other AI evaluation artifacts for public display.

## Before publishing
- Confirm that the project rules permit external sharing.
- Remove names, email addresses, account identifiers, tokens, and other personal or confidential data.
- Remove proprietary prompts, private system instructions, restricted benchmark questions, and client datasets.
- Remove or redact visible private model reasoning.
- Prefer a short, relevant crop over a full-screen dump.
- Ensure the screenshot is legible at typical screen size and provide a full-resolution view when safe.
- State what the artifact demonstrates without implying results that are not visible.
- Separate personal contributions from team or platform work.
- Avoid publishing materials simply because they are in your possession.

## Recommended case-study format
1. Context and objective
2. Your role
3. Workflow and tools
4. Evaluation criteria or quality controls
5. What the evidence demonstrates
6. Limitations and confidentiality note
