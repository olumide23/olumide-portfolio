# Multimodal AI Data Annotation & Validation

## Overview
This case study summarizes experience with AI data preparation and annotation workflows across text, image, video, and audio modalities.

## Workflow
- Interpret task-specific annotation guidelines.
- Apply the required labels or structured fields.
- Check completeness, consistency, and adherence to labeling rules.
- Flag ambiguous examples or guideline conflicts.
- Participate in calibration or quality review when the workflow provides it.
- Document issues clearly for review.

## Modalities
Text, image, video, and audio, subject to the task requirements and the specific work performed.

## Quality principles
Consistency, traceability, careful guideline adherence, accurate labeling, and escalation of unclear cases rather than unsupported assumptions.
